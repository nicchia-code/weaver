# Chat

## general-chat

### View and header
- Its own view in every project window, always present, under the view header (`requirements/ui#General`, title `Chat`), with the job conversation rendering of `requirements/ui#job` (messages, `Thinking`, tool rows, sub-agents, permission cards, queued messages), but no instruction, notes or end line, and with the differences below. The conversation and the message box are centred, at most 760 px wide.
- Every text of the interface names it `Chat`, never `General`: the sidebar entry, its tooltip, the view's title and the texts below. A request refused because it is not open says `Chat is not open for this project`.
- **Agent pill,** right after the title: a pill with a dot and `Weaver · <state>`, from the app-wide state of the agent: `ready` (Sage), `checking`, `installing`, `updating`, `logging in` (Sky), `not installed`, `not connected` (Peach). Its tooltip says what to do when the agent is not ready (`Install it in Settings.`, `Log in to a provider in Settings.`). It never names omp.
- **New chat:** on the right of the header, `New chat` (secondary, `Plus`), disabled while the conversation is empty with `The conversation is empty`; a kept conversation that is loading is not empty. AlertDialog `Start a new chat?` / `The conversation is deleted and a new one starts.` (during a turn: `The agent's turn stops, the conversation is deleted and a new one starts.`), `New chat` (destructive) and `Cancel`, focus on `Cancel`. It is `developer.CLEAR_GENERAL`.
- **Sub-agents:** each sub-agent line opens that sub-agent's conversation in place of the chat, as in `requirements/ui#job` (**Sub-agent conversation**), with the header `Chat › <sub-agent name>`, every parent sub-agent in between, and a `Back to Chat` button (`ArrowLeft`) that returns to the chat at the position it had; that header takes the place of the view header, in the same row and at the same height. Meanwhile the chat goes on; `New chat` also returns to the chat.

### Conversation
- Developer messages: right-aligned boxes on Paper, at most 560 px wide, radius 16 px with a 4 px bottom-right corner.
- Agent messages: a sender row with the logo at 24 px, `Weaver` (13 px semibold) and the time `HH:MM` in Muted, then the Markdown at 14.5 px with a 1.65 line height.
- **Work summary:** every run of consecutive `Thinking` blocks, tool rows and sub-agent lines of a turn is one disclosure, collapsed at first: a Lane box with an inset ring, radius 12 px, three short decorative bars (Lavender, Sky, Sage), `Worked for <duration> · <n> steps` (durations as in `requirements/ui#job` **Times**; `<n>` the tool rows and `Thinking` blocks), and `ChevronDown`, with `aria-expanded`. While the run is the last thing of a turn in progress, it reads `Working · <n> steps` with a Sky running dot. Expanding it shows the rows as `requirements/ui#job` renders them, sub-agent lines included. Permission cards, notes of Weaver and messages are never inside it.
- The backend gives each entry the instant it arrived; `<duration>` goes from the first to the last entry of the run, and the agent message's time is its first text's.
- **Started jobs:** a line `Bot` + `Started <job name>` with `Open conversation`, which selects that job in the Jobs view; the job a request of Chat started shows the same line, with no other line for the request. After Weaver restarts or the conversation is reloaded, the lines of the jobs Chat started (`requirements/data#job`, `byChat`) show again, in creation order, after the replayed conversation, since Weaver stores no conversation text and so not where each line was. A refused start: `Weaver did not start <kind>: <reason>.` in Peach, the kind of a refused request being `design` or `setup`.
- **Cards of questions:** each card of questions of the agent shows in the conversation as `requirements/ui#question-round/Active in General` says, under everything of the turn that published it. While a card is active, the sidebar's Chat entry shows `1` in Peach, with tooltip `Chat has questions for you`; nothing else counts it outside Chat and no notification is raised. The Jobs entry's number never counts Chat.
- Empty: centred secondary text `Think with Weaver about this project. Ask it for a Proposal or a job when you want one.`
- **Loading:** while the kept conversation loads, centred secondary text `Loading conversation…` in place of the empty text, above any message sent meanwhile, until the history shows. When the agent is missing, the `The agent is not installed.` line and `Install the agent` show at once; while it is only checking, updating or not connected, the loading text shows alone.
- **Failures:** `The agent stopped: <reason>.` in Rose, then `Your next message starts the agent again.` in secondary text. With the agent missing: `The agent is not installed.` and the `Install the agent` button.

### Proposal card
- Under the `Started <job name>` line of a job, one card for each Proposal that job handed over, in creation order, from its hand-over on, also after the conversation is reloaded, while the Proposal exists. A Proposal attached to a Drift, decided through its Drift, never has a card here.
- Look: Surface, a 1 px `#E2DDF0` ring and a soft Lavender shadow, radius 14 px. Head, on the Lavender tint with a `#ECE7F6` bottom rule: a 32 px Lavender tile with `FileCheck`, `Proposal <id>` in Lavender ink, the state pill (`Draft`, `Open`, `Refining` in Sky, `Approved` in Sage, `Rejected` in Rose, `Discarded` neutral), the `stale` and `blocked` badges of the Board's card, and the title (16 px semibold).
- Body, 18 px padding: a box `Files` listing the paths it adds, changes and removes (each with `added`, `changed` or `removed` in Muted; at most 6, then `and <n> more`); a box `Creates <n> Tasks` listing their titles (at most 6, then `and <n> more`), only when it carries Tasks; for a blocked Proposal, a Peach box with every blocking reason, one per line, as the Board's `blocked` popover lists them.
- Footer, on Lane after a `#EFEDE7` rule: the controls of the Board's card (`requirements/ui#proposal`) with the same disabled reasons and dialogs: `Approve`, or `Refine` in its place; `Reject` with its menu `Rework with feedback…`; for a Draft left half-way by a refinement, `Refine` and `Discard`. No model control, no `View requirements`, no diff and no undo. At its right end, the Proposal's permanent id in Geist Mono Muted.
- Once decided, the footer becomes one line: `Approved. <n> Tasks are on the Board.` (or `Approved.` without Tasks) in Sage with `Open Board`; `Rejected.`; `Sent back for rework.` with `Open conversation` on the rework job; `Discarded.`. A decision refused when applied shows its reason under the controls in Rose, and nothing changes.

### Message box
- A Surface box with a `#E0DDD5` ring, a soft shadow and radius 16 px, pinned at the bottom of the view over a fade, holding the textarea (placeholder `Message Weaver… use @ to add assets`, accessible name `Message Weaver`) and a row: on the right the model control, then `Send` (`ArrowUp`, a 34 px primary icon button), or `Stop` (no dialog) during a turn. Tags of assets work as in `requirements/ui#assets`; there is no button to attach files and no suggestion chips.
- **Text position:** the text starts 12 px inside the box's own padding (the side padding of a plain `Textarea`), and the caret, the selection and the drawn text all start there, as in the other boxes with tags; the box removes no side padding of its textarea.
- The message box is always enabled; a message sent while the agent starts or reloads shows at once with `Starting Weaver…` under it until delivered.
- **Active card:** while a card of questions is active, a line in secondary text sits right above the box: `Your message also sends the answers chosen so far.`
- **Model control:** a ghost button with the analysis model of the settings, `Default model` when it is empty, and `ChevronDown`; it opens a Popover with the same model choice as the `Analysis` setting (`requirements/ui#developer.CHANGE_SETTINGS`), and a choice is that setting's change, saved at once for every project and shown everywhere. It is disabled with `Change the model when Weaver is not working` during a turn and while the conversation loads; an active card of questions never disables it. Its tooltip says `The model of Chat and of Ask more; it applies to the next message.`
