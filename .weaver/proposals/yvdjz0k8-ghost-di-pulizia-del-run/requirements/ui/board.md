# Board

## work
- Two columns of equal width share the whole view, under the header, with 24 px around and 16 px between them, in this order: `Backlog` (open Proposals and open Drifts in one creation order, oldest first, then the drafts of design requests, oldest first) and `Active` (Tasks in To do and In progress, blocked ones included, oldest first). No Done column, collapsed or not, and no other column.
- Each column is a lane: Lane background, inset 1 px `#EFEDE7` ring, radius 14 px, padding 14 px, 12 px between cards. Its heading: an 8 px dot (Lavender for Backlog, Sky for Active), the title (13 px semibold) and the number of its real cards in Muted; ghosts never count, a resolution card counts once, each draft of a request counts once.
- The Backlog grouping (which Drifts sit in a resolution, which Proposals are hidden) and its drafts of requests come computed from the backend; the interface never regroups and never adds cards of its own.
- No card can be dragged, moved, taken back or removed from the Board. Clicking a card outside its controls, or Enter on its title, opens its details dialog; for a draft of a request it opens the request in the request editor instead (`requirements/ui#design-request`).
- Each column scrolls on its own.
- **Empty Backlog:** with no real card (drafts of requests included) and no ghost in the Backlog, centred in the lane, the woven drawing of the redesign (three threads, decorative), `Nothing to decide` (14 px semibold), `New requests and Proposals from Chat land here for your go-ahead.` in Muted, then `Request` (secondary, `Plus`), which opens a new design request, and `Ask in Chat` (ghost), which shows Chat. **Empty Active:** `Nothing active` in Muted, with no real card and no ghost in Active.
- A ghost hides its column's empty state as a real card does, whatever its kind, Weaver's own ghost of a job included; it still never counts in the column's number, in the header's subtitle or in the sidebar. In the same picture in which a column's last card or ghost leaves, its empty state shows again.

### Header
- Board header (the view header of `requirements/ui#General`, title `Board`), subtitle in Muted: `<n> active · <m> done since last scan`, where `<n>` is the number of real cards in Active and `<m>` the Tasks finished (not closed unchanged) since the last scan job of the whole System started, asked by the developer or started after a merge, deleted and forgotten ones included; each part is left out when 0, and the subtitle with them.
- On its right, in order: `Merge` while Solo mode is on (`requirements/ui#solo-merge`); `More actions`, an icon-only secondary button (`Ellipsis`, tooltip and accessible name `More actions`, always enabled) opening a shadcn `DropdownMenu` whose only item is `Scan System…` (`ScanSearch`), which confirms first (`Compare the whole System with the code?`, `Scan System` / `Cancel`; cancelling leaves the count unchanged); while a merge is in progress the item is disabled and shows `A merge is in progress` under its text in Muted, as menu items carry no tooltip, the reason being also its accessible description; then `New Request` (secondary, `Plus`, tooltip `Write a request in Markdown and send it to an agent`), always enabled, which opens a new design request (see `design-request`); then `Run all` (primary, `Play`) followed inside the button by `<n> ready` in a lighter weight and colour, the Tasks in To do that are not blocked (hidden when 0). Tooltip `Run all the Tasks in To do`. Disabled reasons, in order: `A merge is in progress`, `Drifts are being resolved`, `A run is in progress`, `No Task is in To do`, `All the Tasks in To do are blocked`.
- No column heading holds a control: the Backlog and Active headings show only their dot, title and number.
- While a merge is in progress its banner sits at the top of the Board, above the notices (`requirements/ui#solo-merge`).

### Ghosts
- A ghost sits at the bottom of its target column (Backlog for a Proposal or Drift, Active for a Task), after the real cards, drafts of requests included, in announcement order; Weaver's own ghost of a setup, design or import job, and the cleanup ghost of a Run, take the place they would have if they had been announced when their job was created.
- Skeleton card: dashed border, 70% opacity. Top part, known and still: the kind (`Proposal`, `Task`, `Drift`), the title, a phase line, then the job's latest note in muted text on one line cut with `…` (full text as tooltip). None of these ever shimmers.
- Phase line: `Preparing` until the Draft exists, `Writing` once it exists, `Waiting for your answers` while the job waits for a round or a permission, `Being refined` for a refinement. Plain muted text, no icon, no animation.
- Under the known part, always, one block of placeholders for what the ghost does not know yet, in the order of the real card of its kind and with its shape: muted bars and blocks with no text.
  - Proposal: a short bar for the id, a bar for the files line, a bar for the Tasks line (always shown), then a row of two button-sized blocks for the controls.
  - Task: a small circle for the status dot next to a short bar for the id, then one button-sized block for `Run`.
  - Drift: a short bar for the id, then a row of two button-sized blocks for `Resolve` and `Dismiss Drift`.
- Only the placeholders shimmer: a light band sweeps across them from right to left, once every 2.4 s. Under reduced motion they are still bars.
- The whole ghost is one button that opens its job's conversation in Jobs. Accessible name `<kind> being prepared by the agent: <title>, <phase>`, except for the cleanup ghost of a Run (below).
- When the card arrives, in the same picture the ghost disappears and the card takes its place in creation order.
- **Weaver's own ghost of a setup, design or import job:** from the moment the job is created (queued) until it ends, suspended included, whenever none of the job's announced ghosts is on the Board, Backlog shows one ghost of the job made by Weaver, without any announcement. A request sent from its draft card or from the editor leaves the Backlog in the same picture in which this ghost appears.
  - Kind `Proposal`; title: for a setup or design job the title of the request it received, as its draft card showed it (`requirements/ui#design-request`), for an import job `Import from Code`; phase line `Waiting for your answers` while the job waits for a round or a permission, `Preparing` otherwise (never `Writing`); the job's latest note as on the other ghosts. Same skeleton, button and accessible name as every ghost (`Proposal being prepared by the agent: Checkout redesign, Preparing`); it never counts in the column's number.
  - In the same picture in which the job's first announced ghost appears, it disappears; it comes back in the same picture in which the job's last announced ghost leaves, while the job has not ended. A Proposal's ghost stays while its Draft is written, so Weaver's ghost stays away until that Proposal is handed over.
  - It disappears when the job ends, whatever the reason, in the same picture as the job's other ghosts; when a setup or design job ends without handing over a Proposal bound to its request, the request's draft card comes back in that same picture.
  - Its identity is derived from its job, stays the same across pictures and never equals an announcement's; it is never stored in the job's record.
- **Weaver's cleanup ghost of a Run:** Active shows one ghost made by Weaver, without any announcement, for each Run job (one Task or one Run of a Run all) while all of these hold: the Run has taken at least one Task (`requirements/data#job`, `tookTask`); it holds no Task now; it has not ended, suspended included; Active has no real card (Task in To do or In progress) and no announced ghost of any job; none of the Run's own announced ghosts is on the Board. Cleanup ghosts of several Runs never hide one another.
  - Kind `Task`, with the skeleton and the Task placeholders of every ghost; title `Cleaning up: <job name>` (`Cleaning up: Run T3`, `Cleaning up: All tasks · <model>`); phase line `Waiting for your answers` while the job waits for a round or a permission, `Finishing up` otherwise; the job's latest note as on the other ghosts. Accessible name `Agent cleaning up after its Tasks: <job name>, <phase>` (`Agent cleaning up after its Tasks: Run T3, Finishing up`). Like every ghost it opens its job's conversation and never counts in the column's number.
  - It appears in the same picture in which the Run's last held Task leaves Active, so `Nothing active` never shows in between, and in the same picture in which the last real card or announced ghost leaves Active or the Run's last announced ghost leaves the Board.
  - It disappears in the same picture in which the Run takes a Task, a real card or an announced ghost enters Active, or a ghost announced by the Run appears; and when the job ends, whatever the reason, in the same picture as the job's other ghosts. Between two batches of a Run all it may show briefly.
  - Its identity is derived from its job, stays the same across pictures and never equals an announcement's; it is never stored in the job's record.

### Details dialogs and notices
- A details dialog follows its card: each new picture updates it in place without moving the focus. When the card leaves the Board (decided, Done, deleted, gathered into a resolution) the dialog closes by itself and the focus goes to the column the card was in.
- Notices sit at the top of the Board, above both columns, newest first, each an `Alert` with a close button, until closed or the window closes: decisions refused when applied (`Could not delete T4: the agent that filed it is still working.`), Proposals sent back to Draft while the developer was deciding them, and History entries Weaver did not commit (`requirements/ui#atomic-commits`).
- History warnings sit under the notices, above both columns: one amber `Alert` with `TriangleAlert` for each cited Task whose copy in History could not be made (`Could not record T3 in History: <reason>. The Task stays until its copy can be made.`), each with a close button (`X`, accessible name and tooltip `Close`). A closed warning stays hidden while the window stays open, even when the Board changes; it shows again at the next opening of the window if it is still true. A new warning, or one whose text changed, shows at once.
