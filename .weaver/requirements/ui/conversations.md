# Jobs and conversations

## job
- **Jobs view:** under one header band, the project's jobs listed on the left (Lane background, a `#EFEDE7` rule on its right), the selected job's conversation, or the selected sub-agent's, on the right. Empty list: `No jobs yet`. Ask more conversations are listed among the jobs, with their own row and conversation (see `ask-conversation`).
- **Header band:** the view header's row (`requirements/ui#General`: height, rule, title), split where the list ends, the list's rule going on through it. Left: the title `Jobs` and, at its right end, the icon-only ghost button `Hide jobs list` (`PanelLeftClose`). Right, with 28 px side padding: the header of the selection, the **Job header**, a sub-agent's path or an Ask more conversation's header (`requirements/ui#ask-conversation`); empty without one. With the list hidden, the left part holds only `Jobs` and `Show jobs list` (`PanelLeftOpen`).
- **List width:** 280 px at first; dragging the rule (8 px hit area, `col-resize`) sets it within 240–420 px. The rule is a focusable vertical `separator`, `Resize jobs list`, with its range and value in px: arrows move it 16 px, `Home`/`End` set 240/420 px, `Enter` and a double click 280 px. The conversation pane keeps at least 480 px: in a narrower window the list narrows by itself, down to 240 px, without changing the chosen width. Width and hidden state are remembered per project, across restarts (`requirements/data#window`).
- **Hiding the list:** the right pane then takes the whole width; `Show jobs list` brings the list back as it was. Opening a job or a conversation from elsewhere (Chat, Ask more, a notification, the palette) leaves it hidden.
- **Filter:** at the top of the list pane, a segmented control of three equal parts: `All <n>` (default at each opening of the window), `Active <n>` (jobs queued, running, waiting or suspended, and Ask more conversations whose agent works or waits) and `Ended <n>` (jobs done, failed or stopped). Idle Ask more conversations show only under `All`. A filter that leaves nothing shows `No jobs here` in Muted. The selected job stays selected when the filter hides its row.
- **List spacing:** 12 px of space between the top edge of the jobs list and its first row, inside the list's scrolling content, so it scrolls away with the rows; no added space below the last row.
- **Fixed names**, set at creation: `Setup`, `Design`, `Import`, `Scan`, `Run T3` (short number, else permanent ID), `All tasks`, `All tasks · <model>`, `Resolve D3`, `Rework P3`, `Refine P3`, `Merge conflicts` (kind `merge`); the scan after a merge is `Scan` too. Equal names are told apart by started time.
- **Row:** a 12 px-radius block: name (13.5 px semibold), kind in Muted (`setup`, `design`, `import`, `scan`, `run`, `resolve`, `rework`, `refinement`), the state pill pushed right; then the latest note on up to two lines in Ink 2, cut with `…` (full text in the tooltip); then, in 12 px Muted, the started time and the running time separated by `·`. The selected row stands on Surface with a `#E0DDD5` ring and a soft shadow; the others have no background. No progress percentage, count of changed files or count of checks is shown anywhere: Weaver has no such data.
- **Sub-agent list:** under the selected job's row, and only under it, an indented list of its sub-agents opens; selecting another job closes it and opens that job's. A job with no sub-agents shows no list.
  - Its direct sub-agents in launch order, across all its `task` calls; under each one, indented one more step, the sub-agents it started, in launch order, at any depth. omp's advisors never appear.
  - Each row, one line: `Bot`, omp's name for it truncated with `…` (full name in the tooltip), state as icon and text as in **Sub-agents** below. When siblings have the same name, each of them adds its started time `HH:MM` in secondary text.
  - A row is a button: it shows that sub-agent's conversation on the right and gets `aria-current`; the job's row stays marked as its parent, and selecting the job's row shows the job's conversation again.
  - The list stays, with its states, while a finished job stays listed, also after its session was reloaded; removing the job removes its sub-agents.
- **Badges**, as pills, always with text: `queued` (neutral), `running` (Sky with its running dot; static under reduced motion), `waiting` (Peach, `MessageCircleQuestion`), `suspended` (neutral, `Pause`), `done` (Sage, `Check`), `failed` (Rose, `X`), `stopped` (Rose, `Square`).
- **Times:** started `HH:MM` today, else `D MMM HH:MM`. Running time counts from the session start (none while queued), ticks every second while working, freezes at the end: `45s`, `4m 05s`, `1h 12m`.
- **Order:** waiting, then running, queued, suspended (each newest start first), then finished jobs, latest end first. Ask more rows `waiting` and `running` sort with the jobs in the same state; `idle` ones come after the suspended jobs, newest opening first.
- **Retention:** a finished job stays listed until removed with its row's `X` (no dialog) or the window closes; only the 30 latest finished jobs are kept. Working jobs have no `X`.
- **Job header:** in the right part of the header band: the job's name (17 px semibold) with its state pill and, for a working job, its running time inside the pill (`Running · 2m 16s`); under them in Muted `Started today at HH:MM` (or the date); on the right `View request` (secondary, `FileText`, when shown, see below), then `Stop job` (destructive, `Square`) while the job is queued, running, waiting or suspended.
- **View request:** only on a setup or design job, Chat's included, and only while the request it received exists and is still sent to that job: it is gone once the request is consumed, discarded, deleted or back in writing. It opens that request read-only in the request editor, whose back control `← Jobs` shows this conversation again (`requirements/ui#design-request`).
- **Conversation:** entries top to bottom in a column centred in the right pane, at most 860 px wide, with 22 px 28 px padding, message box pinned at the bottom. The view follows new text unless scrolled up; then `Jump to latest` (`ArrowDown`) appears. Nothing scrolls sideways: tables and code scroll in their own box.
- First entry: Weaver's instruction in a collapsed disclosure `Instructions from Weaver` (Lane, inset ring, radius 12 px); opening it shows the instruction's text.
- **Entries as in Chat** (`requirements/ui#general-chat`, **Conversation**): developer messages, agent messages with their sender row (logo, `Weaver`, time) and text, and the **Work summary** that gathers each run of `Thinking` blocks, tool rows and sub-agent lines; notes, permission cards and the lines below stay outside it. While a run is the last thing of a turn in progress, its summary reads `Working · <n> steps · <latest step>`, the latest step being its last tool row's title, or `Thinking`, on one line cut with `…`.
- **Tool calls:** one compact row each: icon by kind (read `FileText`, search `Search`, command `SquareTerminal`, edit `FilePen`, sub-agent `Bot`, other `Wrench`), omp's title on one line truncated with `…`, status icon (`Loader`, `Check`, `X` in error colour). A row whose text does not fit is a disclosure button (`ChevronRight`/`ChevronDown`, `aria-expanded`) opening the full command (without leading `$ `) or full title in a monospace box, ~12 lines then scrolling. Rows never show results or output. Rows start closed.
- **Sub-agents:** under omp's `task` row, indented, one line each in omp's order: `Bot`, omp's name for it, state as icon and text (`working`, `succeeded`, `failed`, `interrupted`). Each line is a button that opens that sub-agent's conversation and selects it in the sub-agent list. When the job ends, those still working show `interrupted`.
- **Sub-agent conversation:** rendered as a job's conversation (column, entries, its own sub-agents as lines that open theirs), following new text while the sub-agent works, with `Jump to latest` as above.
  - Header: the path from the job, `<job name> › <sub-agent name>`, with every parent sub-agent in between, then the sub-agent's state as icon and text.
  - Its agent messages' sender row shows a 24 px `Bot` tile and the sub-agent's name instead of the logo and `Weaver`.
  - First entry: the assignment it received, shown as a message labelled with the name of who started it (the job's name, or the parent sub-agent's).
  - Read-only: no `Stop`, no permission card, no notes, no end line.
  - **Disabled message box**, for visual consistency with the job's: pinned at the bottom with the same layout as the **Message box**, textarea and `Send` (`ArrowUp`) both disabled, placeholder `Sub-agents take no messages.`, no `Stop`. The same in Chat and Ask more sub-agent conversations, whatever the state of the sub-agent or of its job, Chat or Ask more conversation, also once they have ended (never the `This job has ended…` line). The textarea is really disabled: out of the tab order and announced as unavailable, as in a queued job.
  - A transcript that is missing or cannot be read shows only `Conversation not available: <reason>.` in secondary text, at the top of the conversation, with the disabled message box still at the bottom; the sub-agent stays in the list.
- **Notes:** each note appears where it arrived, one secondary line: `StickyNote`, `HH:MM`, the text.
- **Message box:** as Chat's (`requirements/ui#general-chat`, **Message box**: look, two lines at first, pinned over a fade, text position), centred in the conversation's column, without the model control, growing to ~10 lines, placeholder `Steer the agent while it runs…`, and `Send` (`ArrowUp`, a 34 px primary icon button); `Enter` sends, `Shift+Enter` new line; `Send` is disabled while the text is empty after trimming. While the job works a message reaches the agent without stopping it (steer).
- **Queued message:** one not delivered yet (a round or permission waits, or omp has not taken it) shows `Clock` + `Queued` under it until delivered; a refused one shows `Not delivered: <reason>.` in the error colour; still queued at the job's end: `Not delivered: the job ended.`
- **Stop:** `Stop job` in the job header. AlertDialog `Stop <name>?` / `The agent stops at once and every process it started ends.`, `Stop` (destructive) and `Cancel`, focus on `Cancel`.
- **Permission card** (permissions set to `ask`): a box with a warning border headed `Weaver asks permission`, the compact row of the tool call concerned, then one button per option the agent offers, in the agent's names and order; rejecting options use `outline`. Afterwards it stays read-only with the choice, or `No longer needed.` (voided by the turn's end) or `Cancelled.` (Stop).
- **Queued job:** only `Starting Weaver…` centred in secondary text, or `Waiting for the agent to be ready.` with `Open Settings` while the agent is not ready; `Send` disabled.
- **Suspended job:** a secondary line `Weaver stopped; the job continues when its session is reloaded.`; on resume `Session reloaded; the agent continues its work.`
- **End line:** `Finished.` or `Stopped.` (secondary); failed: `TriangleAlert` + `Failed: <reason>.` in the error colour, reasons in plain words (`omp did not answer within 30 s`, `omp exited with code <N>`, `the session could not be resumed`, `the agent refused`, `the agent ran out of tokens`, `the agent reached its turn limit`, a proxy explanation of `requirements/ui#system-certificates`, or omp's message). After an exit, `Show details` opens omp's last error lines in monospace.
- **Finished conversation:** read-only; in place of the message box, one line centred in the conversation's column, over the fade and with no rule: `This job has ended. Continue in Chat.` with `Open Chat`.
- New agent messages are announced by an `aria-live="polite"` region once per complete message.
