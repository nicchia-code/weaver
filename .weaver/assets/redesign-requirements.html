Stored information

data/stored.md Open in editor
General
  • Daily counters of submitted documents per guest session, per network address and per account, per calendar day; counters older than 2 days are deleted.
session
  • Guest session: a random identifier, creation time.
  • Account: provider (Google or Microsoft), the provider's subject identifier, email, display name, creation time, time of the last sign-in.
document
  • Owner (a guest session or an account), title, level (mild, medium, strong), state, creation time, time of the last change, start time of the current processing, reason of the last failure.
  • Pages, in order, with their kind of file and size, until the document reaches Review.
  • The content read from the pages, until the document reaches Review.
  • The simplified blocks in order, each with its kind and text, and for a figure its image and caption.
  • The PDF is never stored: it is built at each download.
